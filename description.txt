Data Generation Approach: Enterprise FinTech SaaS Marketing Analytics

1. Scenario Selection:
The dataset was modeled after a real-world Enterprise FinTech SaaS operation. This specific industry was chosen to demonstrate high-stakes, precision-driven marketing where audience quality (CFOs, VP-level finance decision makers) is more critical than raw click volume.

2. Strategic Storytelling & Seasonality:
The data follows a deliberate 12-month narrative (Jan-Dec 2023) rather than random values:
- Fiscal Year-End "Budget Flush": A massive spike in November Programmatic spend ($10,841,029) simulates enterprise clients closing software contracts before the new fiscal year.
- Strategic Optimization: The data reflects a clear shift in budget from Paid Social (identified as an audience mismatch) toward high-intent Paid Search and LinkedIn ABM (Account-Based Marketing) campaigns.
- Industry Trends: High CPCs ($6.1K) and premium CPMs ($487K) reflect the competitive nature of financial keyword auctions.

3. Channel & Source Logic:
Each channel follows a specific behavioral profile:
- Programmatic: High impressions but volatile, with a massive end-of-year burst via DV360 and Amazon Ad Server (which shows a 142.7% growth outlier tied to the Nov push).
- Paid Search: Steady growth throughout the year, representing sustained demand generation through Google Search Ads 360.
- Video Strategy: A sharp spike in Video Views during Q3 (August) models the launch of a premium financial thought-leadership content series.
- LinkedIn Ads: Optimized for quality over volume, showing high CTR (9.87%) and conversion rates targeting executive job titles.

4. Technical Implementation:
The dataset was hand-crafted within TypeScript (`mockData.ts`) to ensure 100% internal consistency across KPI cards, time-series trends, and multi-dimensional tables. Sparklines were precisely mapped to the monthly narrative, ensuring that the visual "story" in the dashboard aligns perfectly with the strategic marketing logic described above.
